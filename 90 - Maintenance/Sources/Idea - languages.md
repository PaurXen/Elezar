Good idea: make **Divine Latin** the original sacred tongue, then each race speaks a “descendant” or “corrupted/refined” form shaped by land, biology, and culture.

Base divine language:

> **Divina Lingua / Lingua Dei** — sacred Latin-like language of gods, thrones, magic, and old inscriptions.

Then race languages can be Romance-inspired, but stylized.

---

# Core approach

Use the same Latin roots, but each race changes them differently.

# Languages 
## Dwarves — harsh, stone-heavy

### Best inspiration

Use **Romanian + Sardinian + Old Latin**, then make it harder.

Real Romance inspirations:

- Romanian has harsher consonants and Slavic influence.
    
- Sardinian is conservative and archaic.
    
- Old Latin feels rougher than polished Classical Latin.
    

### Sound style

Dwarven Latin should use:

- hard **k, g, d, t, r**
    
- many consonant clusters
    
- short words
    
- endings like **-ak, -az, -or, -ur, -um**
    
- rolled or guttural **r**
    
- less flowing vowels
    

### Examples

|Divine Latin|Meaning|Dwarven form|
|---|---|---|
|**mons**|mountain|**Morn / Morz**|
|**saxum**|stone|**Sakzum / Sakhum**|
|**ignis**|fire|**Ignar / Igrun**|
|**deus**|god|**Dez / Daus**|
|**urbs**|city|**Urbak / Urgaz**|
|**ferrum**|iron|**Ferruk / Ferraz**|

### Sample phrase

Divine Latin:

> **Mons ferri stat aeternus.**  
> The mountain of iron stands eternal.

Dwarven:

> **Morz ferraz stat urnak.**

Feeling: hard, old, carved into stone.

Possible language names:

- **Karazic**
    
- **Ferrunic**
    
- **Durnic**
    
- **Saxar**
    
- **Urgnoric**
    
- **Karaz-Latin**
    
- **Lingua Ferri** — “language of iron”
    

My favorite: **Karazic** or **Ferrunic**.






## Elves — soft, elegant, forest-like

### Best inspiration

Use **Italian + Occitan + Old French**, but smoother.

Real Romance inspirations:

- Italian is melodic and vowel-rich.
    
- Occitan feels lyrical and poetic.
    
- Old French gives noble/ancient elegance.
    

### Sound style

Elven Latin should use:

- many vowels
    
- soft **l, m, n, s, v**
    
- softened consonants
    
- endings like **-ia, -iel, -ae, -elle, -ion**
    
- avoid harsh clusters
    
- words should feel sung
    

### Examples

|Divine Latin|Meaning|Elven form|
|---|---|---|
|**silva**|forest|**Sylvia / Silvae**|
|**luna**|moon|**Lunelle / Lúnia**|
|**aqua**|water|**Aquia / Avelle**|
|**deus**|god|**Deo / Deael**|
|**somnium**|dream|**Somnia / Somniel**|
|**flos**|flower|**Floria / Floel**|

### Sample phrase

Divine Latin:

> **Silva somnium lunae cantat.**  
> The forest sings the dream of the moon.

Elven:

> **Sylvia somniel lunae canta.**

Feeling: soft, old, magical, poetic.

Possible language names:

- **Sylvan Latin**
    
- **Silvarian**
    
- **Lunelian**
    
- **Eldarin**
    
- **Doriathic**
    
- **Lingua Silvae** — “language of the forest”
    
- **Somniel**
    

My favorite: **Doriathic** or **Silvarian**.

## Humans — neutral, practical, common

### Best inspiration

Use **Spanish + Italian + simplified Vulgar Latin**.

Real Romance inspirations:

- Spanish is strong but clear.
    
- Italian is close to Latin and readable.
    
- Vulgar Latin gives “common speech” feeling.
    

### Sound style

Human language should be:

- understandable,
    
- balanced,
    
- not too harsh or too soft,
    
- good for trade, law, empire, roads, armies,
    
- simplified grammar compared to Divine Latin.
    

### Examples

|Divine Latin|Meaning|Human form|
|---|---|---|
|**deus**|god|**Dios / Deo**|
|**mundus**|world|**Mundo**|
|**urbs**|city|**Urba / Orba**|
|**rex**|king|**Rey / Reo**|
|**terra**|land|**Terra**|
|**via**|road|**Via**|

### Sample phrase

Divine Latin:

> **Rex mundum unit.**  
> The king unites the world.

Human:

> **Rey mundo unita.**

Feeling: imperial, clear, usable.

Possible language names:

- **Asedolic**
    
- **Common Alfean**
    
- **Imperial**
    
- **Vulgar Divine**
    
- **Lingua Communis**
    
- **Mundan**
    
- **Asedol Latin**
    

My favorite: **Asedolic** for the imperial human language, and **Lingua Communis** for common speech.

## Dragons — powerful, ancient, commanding

### Best inspiration

Use **Classical Latin + Old Latin + Romanian/Sardinian hardness**, but make it grand and archaic.

Dragons should sound like they speak a version closer to Divine Latin, but heavier and older.

### Sound style

Dragon language should use:

- long vowels,
    
- deep consonants,
    
- preserved Latin forms,
    
- strong **r, x, dr, gr, ct**
    
- words that feel like titles or laws,
    
- fewer soft changes than other races.
    

They should sound like living monuments.

### Examples

|Divine Latin|Meaning|Dragon form|
|---|---|---|
|**draco**|dragon|**Drakō / Dracon**|
|**ignis**|fire|**Ignis / Ygnar**|
|**rex**|king|**Rex / Rhaex**|
|**caelum**|sky|**Kaelum / Caelur**|
|**aeternus**|eternal|**Aetern / Aeternum**|
|**potestas**|power|**Potesthar / Potestas**|

### Sample phrase

Divine Latin:

> **Draco ignem caeli portat.**  
> The dragon carries the fire of heaven.

Dragon:

> **Drakō ignar kaelum portat.**

Or more grand:

> **Drakō Aeternum Ignem Portat.**

Feeling: ancient, royal, terrifying.

Possible language names:

- **Draconic Latin**
    
- **Draconian**
    
- **Rexian**
    
- **Ignarian**
    
- **Caeluric**
    
- **Lingua Draconum** — “language of dragons”
    
- **High Draconic**
    

My favorite: **Lingua Draconum** as formal name, **Draconian** as common name.

## Sea elves — fluid, wave-like

Real Romance inspirations:

- Portuguese has nasal, fluid, oceanic sound.
    
- Galician is close to Portuguese and soft.
    
- Venetian has smooth coastal trade-city feeling.

Sea elf language should use:

- flowing vowels,
    
- nasal sounds,
    
- soft **m, n, lh, v, s**
    
- endings like **-ão, -ia, -iel, -mar, -ae**
    
- many words linked to water, moon, tides, memory.

# One phrase in all languages

Phrase:

> **God dreamed the world.**

Divine Latin:

> **Deus mundum somniavit.**

Human / Asedolic:

> **Dios mundo soñavit.**

Elven / Doriathic:

> **Deo mundiel somnia.**

Dwarven / Karazic:

> **Dez mundur somnavt.**

Dragon / Draconian:

> **Deus Mundum Somniavit.**  
> or  
> **Rhae-Deus Mundum Somniavit.**

Sea elf / Marelian:

> **Deo mundo sonhou.**  
> or fantasy-styled:  
> **Deo maer-mundo somia.**

My strongest set for Elezar:

```text
Divine: Deus mundum somniavit.
Human: Dios mundo soñavit.
Elven: Deo mundiel somnia.
Dwarven: Dez mundur somnavt.
Dragon: Deus Mundum Somniavit.
Sea elf: Deo mundo sonhou.
```

That gives every race a shared Latin root, but each one feels different.

- **Divina Lingua** — sacred Primordial / old divine language
- **Asedolic** — human / imperial / common
- **Karazic** — dwarven / stone-ore language
- **Doriathic** — elven / forest language
- **Draconian** — dragon / ancient high speech
- **Marelian** — merfolk / sea language
- **Hortelic** — halfling / field-grove language
- **Altogarn** — goliath / highland language
