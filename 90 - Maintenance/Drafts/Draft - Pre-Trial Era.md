### [[Alfeos]]
#### [[Dwarves]]
They emerged mainly from the [[NW-Mountains]] and [[SW-Mountains]], although they appeared on elven [[S-Mountains]]. [[Dwarves]] prefaded to stay in their mountains and burrow in earth so except mountains, ore reach terrains and some farm lands their territory is small. 
Dwarves tried to extend their lands into the [[Final Mountains]] but [[Dragons]] destroyed their settlements leaving only few ruins. Later dwarves from [[NW-Mountains]] and [[SW-Mountains]] _"united"_ under the banner of [[Karaz-a-Karak]] which cause a feud with [[Humans]] cause dwarven lands ware separated by cities of [[Asedolon]].
Capital was set to be city of [[Dwarven Capital]] witch was renamed after their new nation [[Karaz-a-Karak]] and their second capital became city of [[Obsidianhold]].
Dwarves culture resolves around earth, ores and gems and worship them. That's why many think that other races desecrate lands blessings. Before Kingdom of [[Asedolon]] established [[currency]] based on predacious metals trade of goods between races sparked a lot of conflicts with [[Dwarves]] attacking human settlements. 
[[Dwarves]] leaving inside [[S-Mountains]] are leaving in harmony with [[Elves]] and throughout manny years they adapted to [[Doriathu Culture|elven culture]] and developed some of the elvish traits such as longer ears and slimer build.
As their religion revolves around ores they worship [[Dreamveil]] as sacred object. Because of that dwarves consider silver-purple-gold as colors of wealth which translates to their architecture and style with houses painted in white-gray, yellow and purple(purple being most wealthy because of the rarity) and silverish garments. Aside from mines themselves they highly care about tidiness and especially a shine but not sparkling one. Their eyes adapted to darkness so to not overwhelm themselves with sparlings they mastered the art of light painting and reflections creating breathtaking spectacles.
